A few useful details

Questions are welcome — here are the ones we hear most.

How do I get pricing?

Build your quote list above, add your contact details and submit. We will confirm pricing, availability, pack quantities and delivery with you directly.

Can I request cases or bulk quantities?

Yes. Set the quantity you need and include any preferred case or pack sizes in the item notes, then submit. We will confirm the available options.

Where do you deliver?

Tell us where the job is in your request notes — we confirm delivery options, timing and any shipping cost right in your quote, before anything is ordered.

Does this site accept orders or payments?

No. The site lets you browse departments and send a quote request directly to CCC Supplys. Orders and payments are arranged with us after your quote is confirmed.

Can I just call or text my list instead?

Absolutely. Call or text (346) 337-9514 — or our Arizona line, (928) 227-2868 — and read us the list. Same fast follow-up.
