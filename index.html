Straight answers

Questions, answered.

Why don't I see prices?

Supply prices move with the market. Rather than post numbers that go stale, we price your list live the day you send it — that's how you get the real bulk rate.

Can I order in bulk?

Yes — case, pallet and crew-size quantities are the norm here. Put the quantity on each item and we'll price it that way.

How fast do I get my quote?

Send the list and we follow up as soon as we've confirmed availability, pricing and delivery. Need it faster? Call or text (346) 337-9514.

Can I just call or text my list?

Absolutely — call or text (346) 337-9514, or our Arizona line (928) 227-2868, and we'll build the quote with you.
